Orenucopia is a mod focused on expanding the caving experience by adding new 3d ore outcrops on world generation as well as new and rare minerals to collect.\
Find the new "prospecting" enchantment for pickaxes to dive into the quality of life (QoL) and the mod's mineral system.


## Features

- 8 new blocks with at least 2 size variants each.
- 9 minerals (items) to collect from outcrops and ores.
- A new enchantment greatly increasing the chances to obtain minerals.
- A scrapping mechanic to recycle unwanted gear and tools and earn back scraps to repair or reinforce any item with durability.
- Cosmetic upgrades to tools in the form of pride themed tourmalines changing the particles effects when mining blocks.
- And more!

Learn more on the mod's wiki page (currently in development)!

![Collage of 5 minecraft cave screenshots featuring various ore outcrops](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/394e095cb523849c5ce4d0cd22e7d6e0c2d64815.webp)

## Compatibility and Use

Orenucopia has compatibility with the [Create](https://modrinth.com/mod/create) mod's zinc ores.\
Some features may not work as intended with mods affecting the durability of tools and gear.

And lastly, feel free to use in your modpacks!
